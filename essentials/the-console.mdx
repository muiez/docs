The **console** is the LeedAB workspace, the screen you open in the morning. It is built so a leader can see the state of the operation and act on it in one place.

## The command nav

The COO lives top-left on every screen as a command mark. From it you get:

- **What needs your call**, surfaced the moment you open the workspace and held until it is resolved.
- A **focus command bar** to ask a question or issue an instruction, with the COO drafting the exact action and answering from live data.
- A live view of **what the team is working on**.

Because it rides every screen, you never have to go looking for the COO to act.

## Your operators

Each operator has its own surface for the work in its area. Open one to read its view, ask it something, or hand it a task. It proposes actions you confirm, and hands off to the COO or another operator when a job crosses authority.

## One place for the operation

Alongside the team, a workspace gives you the shared **documents and knowledge** your operators read from, and the **audit trail** of everything that has happened. The numbers on every surface come from the same live source, so what an operator tells you always matches what the screen shows.
