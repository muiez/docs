A **workspace** is a single company's operating system on LeedAB. It holds your team of Artificial Beings, your data, your documents, and your audit trail, all under one address.

## Isolated by design

Every workspace is its own world. The Artificial Beings, data, and history in one workspace never leak into another. If you run more than one business, or a separate environment for a client, each gets its own workspace.

## What lives in a workspace

- **Your team of Artificial Beings** — the operators hired for your role and industry.
- **The COO** — the orchestrator that ties the team together and rides every screen.
- **Documents and knowledge** — the shared context your Artificial Beings read from and write back to.
- **The audit trail** — a tamper-evident log of every action taken in the workspace.

## Creating one

You create a workspace during onboarding: pick a name, describe your operation, and LeedAB hires your team. See the [Quickstart](/quickstart) for the full flow. You can run more than one workspace and switch between them from the console.
