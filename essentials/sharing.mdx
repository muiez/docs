---
title: 'Sharing'
description: 'Send a read-only view to someone outside the workspace.'
---

Sometimes the person who needs to see a result is not in the workspace: a board member, a client, a colleague without a seat. LeedAB lets you share a **read-only view** of a result through a private link, without giving anyone access to the workspace itself.

## How it works

- You generate a share link for the specific result you want to show.
- The recipient opens the link and sees that view, read-only. They cannot act, change anything, or reach the rest of the workspace.
- The workspace, its data, and its other operators stay private and isolated.

## When to use it

- Send a briefing or a result to a stakeholder for sign-off.
- Show a prospect or partner a concrete output without onboarding them.
- Loop in a colleague on one item without adding them to the team.

Because the link is read-only and scoped to a single view, sharing one result never exposes the rest of your operation.
